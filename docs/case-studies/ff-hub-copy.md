## The problem

**A content library at Fortune 500 scale, with the engagement of a 2014 intranet.**

60% of users left after their first session. Most who returned hit the same three pages, every visit. The product wasn't broken — it was the wrong product.

Three constraints framed every decision:

- **Compliance.** Cannot give individualized financial advice. Every recommendation reads as guidance, not prescription.
- **Multi-tenant.** Every employer's benefits, brand, and audience differs. The system has to feel coherent across all of them.
- **Trust.** AI in financial guidance is a liability if it hallucinates. Open-internet AI was off the table.

---

## The opportunity

*What if financial wellness adapted to a person — without ever feeling like surveillance, and without ever sounding like the open internet?*

| Welcome screen | → | Wellness score in 90 seconds |
| Static dashboard | → | Personalized action plan |
| Browse content | → | Complete milestones, watch the score climb |
| Generic chatbot | → | Aimee AI — trained only on FF + our coaches |
| Benefits in HR portal | → | Benefits surfaced at the moment of need |

---

## The solution

**A score-driven behavior change platform with a hybrid coaching layer.**

Six pieces:

01. **Financial wellness assessment.** Five-minute entry. Outputs a score from 1 to 10.
02. **Personalized action plan.** Specific tasks tied to the score and the user's life.
03. **Milestones that move the score.** Complete a task, the score climbs, the next task unlocks.
04. **Aimee AI — safe by training.** Built on FF's content, proprietary data, and human-coach transcripts. Not the open internet. Every answer sourceable.
05. **Live human coaches.** Bookable from any page. Aimee hands off; coaches hand back.
06. **Integrated benefits.** Employer benefits surface inside the action plan and Aimee's responses — at the moment they're useful.

*Aimee doesn't quote the internet. She quotes our coaches.*

## Process

**The Hub didn't get redesigned in Figma. It got redesigned in research.**

A six-week discovery sprint: 14 employer pilots, 200+ employee interviews, three diary studies. The synthesis produced a framework — five life paths, three engagement modes, two compliance lanes — that engineering, sales, and compliance all built against. Decisions made once, reused everywhere.
