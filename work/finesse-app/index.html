01 · At a glance

Built from years of real coaching conversations

I studied years of conversations between our coaches and the people they help, then built the app around what those people want and do every day.

The problem

Money apps hand you charts and a list of goals. You still have to work out what to do today.

The bet

A coach that knows you, prepares the move, and asks for one tap. Closer to a financial therapist than an advisor.

My role

Personas, product design, the full prototype in code, and Aimee's conversation design, with our AI engineers on the agent.

Research

Personas drawn from years of coach and member interactions

Built with

React · TypeScript · Motion · a Cloudflare Worker · Claude for live Aimee, with scripted fallbacks · built with Claude Code

Status

Clickable prototype for leadership, October 2026

02 · The brief I wrote

Two rules for every screen

Lots of apps already set goals. I made the bet on knowing the person, and on making the next move obvious.

"This knows me."

The first headline on Today is always about you: your money type, your goals, your week. Never about the app.

"I know what's next."

One next step a day, and it always moves a goal. Every card is a tap that gets something done or opens Aimee.

03 · Onboarding

Four questions and your accounts, and it knows you

You connect your accounts and answer four questions about people and paydays, not percentages. Then the app names your money type.

04 · Every day

One next step, and a reason to come back

Today leads with you, then the step: "You're 5 paydays to debt free." A daily question teaches one money idea at a time.

05 · The muse pattern

Aimee does the work. You make the call.

Aimee spots the move, prepares it, and shows you before and after. Nothing moves without your OK, and she logs every action.

When the company connects

Aimee makes the switch for you.

When it doesn't

She gives you the steps and an "I did it" button, and can draft a note asking them to connect.

06 · Insight

It matches how you felt to what you spent

Each day asks how you feel about your purchases. Aimee lines those check-ins up with your spending and shows you the pattern.

07 · Hard days

A different voice for hard news, and a real coach a tap away

Aimee sounds excited about a boat and gentle about a separation. When you want a person, Anita, a CFP® coach, joins the chat with Aimee's notes.

08 · Design decisions

Four calls that shaped it

Show one money type

Two labels, who you are and who you should be, read like a grade. You get one type and choose whether to grow toward another.

Approval cards over autopilot

The design assumes Aimee can move money. Every move still shows Now and After and waits for your tap.

The app's cards inside the chat

Aimee writes short replies and embeds real goal, transaction, and approval cards, so you always know what she means.

One accent, glass for chrome only

Navy marks the one action on each card. Content sits on solid white. A test checks every text pair for AA contrast in light and dark.

I turned years of coaching conversations into an app that knows the person holding it. Leadership can tap through the product instead of reading a deck about it.
