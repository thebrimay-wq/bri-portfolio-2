The Hub, rebuilt around each member.

The Hub did its job, but the design was dated and it had no AI. I rebuilt it as a personal platform with an AI that adapts to each member, tested it with users myself, and we launched by the January 1 deadline.

2.5 weeks
From a one-page concept to the full design
1.5 months
With dev, to a build ready for testing
+200%
Engagement after launch, per internal reporting
01 · Before

It worked, but it looked its age

Members could find what the Hub offered. Nothing in it changed for the person using it, and there was no AI.

02 · The structure

One page from another designer, three columns kept

The concept was a single page. I kept only its three columns and built them out for everything the Hub offers: the member's progress and next steps on the left, their priorities and content in the middle, and Aimee on the right. That took two and a half weeks.

03 · Build and test

Design, build and testing in the same sprints

I worked with our developers on the build and ran the user testing myself on UserTesting. When sessions showed a problem, I changed the design and the team built the change in the next sprint. It was all hands on deck.

5

Rounds of UserTesting, at different stages of the build

48

People tested in all

04 · Personal

It adapts to each member

The assessment shapes the home screen. Content comes grouped by the problems people bring, and Aimee, our AI coach, answers inside the Hub and suggests a next step.

05 · Launch

Live by January 1, and next, every market

We hit the deadline, and engagement rose after launch. Now Global Content Studio and the localization pipeline I built are taking the Hub's content to other countries, with 70 planned by the end of 2026.

I turned a one-page concept into the whole Hub, built and tested it alongside our developers, and shipped on time. The AI work that followed is built on it.
