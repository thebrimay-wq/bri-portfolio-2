From content library to guided plan.

The Hub was a library that made members do the work. In 2024 I led its redesign from research through launch: a short assessment, a personal action plan as the home screen, recommended content, and a coach within reach.

01 · At a glance

Members landed on a library and had to decide what to read

The redesign gave them a starting point: answer a few questions, get a plan, see the next action, and reach a coach from any page.

The problem

A static library with no starting point, no personalization, and no next step. Reading an article isn't the outcome; doing something about your money is.

The bet

Build the product around the member's plan, not around the content.

My role

Lead Product Designer: strategy, design direction, the cross-surface design system, and the flows, from research through launch.

Team

Product, engineering, and enterprise account stakeholders

Screens

From the current Hub (2026). The assessment, plan-first home, and curated content are the structure the 2024 redesign set.

02 · First screen

Personal from the first question

A short assessment replaces the generic welcome. By the time a member reaches home, it's already about them.

03 · Content

Organized by the problems people bring

Rows like "Don't make these financial mistakes" mix articles, calculators, and videos, so the right next thing can be any of them.

04 · Later, 2025 – 26

The AI coach ends every answer with an action

Built on the 2024 plan structure, not part of it. Aimee answers inside the Hub, then offers a step the member can add to their plan.

05 · How I led it

A rethink of what the product was for

A business driver

Not a visual refresh. The redesign had to change what members do next.

Behavior first

The information architecture follows the member's plan instead of the content catalog.

One system, two platforms

A cross-surface design system kept web and mobile consistent and fast to ship. The later Aimee and Studio work built on it.

Research to launch

Design led from research through launch with product and engineering, close to enterprise clients the whole way.
