02 · The problem

Reading an article isn't the outcome

Doing something about your money is. The library had no starting point, nothing personal, and no next step.

03 · Research

What research showed

I started from years of our coaches' research, then ran a first round of testing with members.

04 · Information architecture

The plan, not the catalog

I moved the library a level down. Home became the member's plan: a short assessment, three priorities, and the next action, with content recommended against each one.

05 · With our CEO

Eight direction changes. The plan stayed the home screen.

I prototyped the first version of the Hub in 24 hours. Through the redesign, I worked each change of direction with our CEO and prototyped it so we could react to something real.

08 · Impact

What came next was built on it

Engagement rose after launch, per internal reporting. The AI work that followed, Aimee and Global Content Studio, was built on this structure.

The Hub went from a library members had to search to a plan that tells them what to do next. Our AI products sit on that plan today.
