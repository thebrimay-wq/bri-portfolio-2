The Problem

Access to content isn't the same as guidance

Keyword search required people to already know what to ask — and most didn't know where to start.
There was no connection between content and an employee's actual benefits, and sessions ended without a clear next step.
Generic AI wasn't an option — financial decisions are high-stakes, and the product had to be trustworthy enough for employers to embed.

Process

Designing under compliance constraint

In financial wellness, the hard part isn't what the AI can say — it's what it must not. I designed Aimee for a bounded, sourced, on-topic experience, treating compliance as the design problem rather than a checkbox at the end. That constraint is exactly what made Aimee trustworthy enough for employers to embed.

Generic open-domain chatbot → CFP-backed, constrained coach
Maximize what the AI can say → Design for what it must not
Answers from the open internet → Bounded, sourced, on-topic
Trust as an afterthought → Trust as the core requirement

The constraint wasn't a limitation — it was the design. Trust is what made Aimee embeddable at all.

What I'd do next

Aimee proved the model. Here's where it goes further.

01

Proactive, not reactive

Move from answering questions to anticipating needs — surfacing timely nudges before an employee thinks to ask.

02

Deeper benefits personalization

Act on real plan data, not just profile inputs — so guidance reflects exactly what each employee is enrolled in.

03

Continuity across the journey

Remember context between sessions so Aimee feels like an ongoing relationship, not a one-off chat.

04

Measurable behavior change

Tie coaching to real financial outcomes — not just engagement — to prove the guidance actually moves the needle.

Final Takeaway

Aimee proved that in a high-stakes domain, trust is the product. By grounding every answer in CFP expertise and designing for what the AI must not say, a static Q&A tool became a coach reaching 1M+ employees. Constraint, designed well, is what makes AI trustworthy enough to ship.
